# Independent body requests reuse single-page admission

Untouched future pins fix `single-admission-body-pairs/v1`. After the ordinary
single-page evidence, isolation, scope, current identity and full-input checks,
the controller records a hash-bound, operation-local admission fact. Pairing
reuses it; it is not a model judgment, semantic approval, budget grant or release
qualification. Missing/denied/changed admissions cannot borrow another page's.

Two independently admitted text pages can share one request irrespective of
topic, source, applicability, shared evidence or QA labels. Keep each page's
original materials, citations, QA notes, uncertainty, parent, conditions and
exceptions intact. Exact duplicate evidence may share a lossless pool; absence
of deduplication or a positive byte estimate does not prohibit pairing.
The final complete request and output headroom must fit the pinned budget.
If A+B does not fit, B remains eligible for B+C; A alone does not force B alone.

Only fresh pages pair. Partial failure preserves success and dispatches an
eligible correction only for the failed page under the same cumulative ledger.
Raw-response capture, per-page durable receipts and stopped replay keep the
existing exact-request/native-completion proof. Old consumed contracts remain
reconstructible and do not gain another allowance.

Asset links alone are not image transport. A page already admitted for a text
request keeps its existing required-reading decision and all linked metadata.
Actual image payloads stay single until exact source/hash/slot binding and cheap
reuse of the existing single-page media proof are established. Pairing itself
does not open asset files or invoke a model to decide whether QA/assets matter.
Measure actual asset reads, bytes and time separately; real pair-vs-concurrent-
single wait/token/output/quality comparisons need separate native authorization.

Use `python3 "<skill-dir>/scripts/compare_single_admission_pairs.py" --vault <vault>
--workflow-id <id> --output <outside-vault-report>` for a read-only fixed-plan
comparison. Keep40000 constant: original eligibility, remove only QA splitting,
then reuse all text-single admissions. The final row additionally includes the
new generation-only instructions and pairing pin in the complete request.
Single inputs prepare once per comparison, with no pair-driven asset rescans.
Counts of overlapping QA pair judgments are not saved model calls. Historical
outcomes/budgets stay untouched; this is a counterfactual plan before outcomes,
never authority to resend the existing workflow's completed or isolated pages.
